### From Harm to Healing: Understanding Individual Resilience after Cybercrimes
*Xiaowei Chen, Mindy Tran, Yue Deng, Bhupendra Acharya, Yixin Zou (2026)*

The article "Individual Cyber Resilience: Understanding Resiliency after Cybercrime" aims to present a conceptual understanding of cyber resilience and its relationship to various types of cybercrime. The authors provide a clear definition of cyber resilience as the ability to resist against cybercrime, recover from cybercrime that targets individuals, and function continuously despite cybercrime. They also propose a human-centered approach to create better support infrastructure for cybercrime victims. The paper presents a review of cybercrime types, discusses their negative impacts on victims, and presents a preliminary definition of cyber resilience. The core contribution of this paper is the conceptual understanding of cyber resilience and its relationship to cybercrime. The methodology includes a systematic review of the literature on cybercrime, quantitative analysis of the empirical data, and discussion of the results. The article lists 3-5 key findings as short bullet points, each on its own line prefixed with '-'.
