### ChainSplitter: Towards Blockchain-based Industrial IoT Architecture for Supporting Hierarchical Storage
*Gang Wang, Zhijie Jerry Shi, Mark Nixon, Song Han (2019)*

Title: ChaingSplitter: Towards Blockchain-based Industrial IoT Architecture for Supporting Hierarchical Storage Abstract: The fast-evolving Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) technologies offer a promising opportunity to build large-scale systems to connect numerous heterogeneous devices and systems into the Internet, but the required storage space poses a graft challenge to resource-constrained IIoT infrastructures. In this paper, the authors propose a blockchain-based IIoT architecture that provides immutable and veriﬁable services for large-scale IIoT systems by storing data in the cloud, while maintaining records in local IIoT networks. The proposed hierarchical blockchain storage structure allows the majority of the blockchain to be stored in the cloud, while the most recent blocks are stored in the overlay network of the individual IIoT networks. To ensure efﬁciency of the proposed hierarchical blockchain storage, the authors provide a case study and showcase the efficiency of the proposed hierarchical blockchain storage in a practical Industrial IoT case. The authors propose a novel blockchain storage structure, called ChaingSplitter, to store the blocks in a hierarchical manner. This contribution, methodology, and results provide a novel solution for supporting immutable and veriﬁable services in large-scale IIoT systems by leveraging the advantages of blockchain technology. The authors' findings include the efﬁciency and scalability of the proposed hierarchical blockchain storage, which can support large-scale IIoT systems.
